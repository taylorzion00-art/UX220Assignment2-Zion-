# My First Job at a Bakery

## What was it that I did?

My first job was at a bakery. I was hired to help customers at the front, but most of the time I got sent to the back to do physical work instead.

## When did I do it?

About four years ago. I was 16.

## What was good about it?

Free food, for one. There was always something to eat. The people I worked with were also around my age and pretty cool, so shifts didn't drag as much.

## What was my favourite memory of it?

Sometimes the bakers would try out new recipes and get me to taste them before anything went out to customers. I'd tell them if it was good or not. It was cool being the first person to try something new.

## What didn't I enjoy about it?

My supervisor. They weren't easy to work for. I wanted to be out front helping customers, but they kept putting me on physical labour instead.

## Grade: C

It was ok. Not bad for a first job, but the supervisor made it hard to enjoy.
