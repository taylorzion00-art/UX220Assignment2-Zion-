# A Day at Canada's Wonderland

## What was it that I did?
I went to Canada's Wonderland with my sister and some friends. The plan was to go on rides and check out the different attractions around the park.

## When did I do it?
Last summer.

## What was good about it?
Honestly, the only good part was that there were some cool people to talk to.

## What was my favourite memory of it?
I don't really have one. It just wasn't a fun experience.

## What didn't I enjoy about it?
Pretty much everything. I ended up paying for things I didn't even need to pay for, I never got a chance to go on a single ride, and the people I was with were really annoying the whole day.

## Grade: F
Still having nightmares. I spent a full day and a bunch of money there and didn't get to do the one thing I actually went for.
