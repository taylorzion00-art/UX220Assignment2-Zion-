# Building My Own Roblox Game

## What was it that I did?
I've been building my own game in Roblox Studio. I've been learning how to script and put the different pieces of the game together, and I'm planning to start using Blender to make better-quality models for it.

## When did I do it?
I started this fall and I'm still working on it.

## What was good about it?
Seeing something I came up with actually work. It also connects to what I'm studying in UX, since I have to think about how players will move through the game and what makes it easy or frustrating to play.

## What was my favourite memory of it?
The first time I playtested it and something I built worked exactly the way I pictured it in my head.

## What didn't I enjoy about it?
Debugging. One small thing breaks and it can take forever to figure out why. Learning Blender on top of Roblox Studio is also a lot to take on at once.

## Grade: B
For the most part enjoyable. It gets frustrating, but it feels good when everything finally comes together.
